# Vertical Playbooks

Use when seeding `site-content.json` and writing first-pass copy. Adapt city/name from user input.

## Roofer

**Services:** Roof repair, full replacement, storm damage inspection, gutter install, commercial roofing  
**Hero angle:** Protect your home before the next storm — free inspection  
**Form fields:** roof type, damage type (leak/storm/age), stories, insurance claim (yes/no)  
**Trust:** Licensed, insured, manufacturer certifications, workmanship warranty  
**Gallery:** Before/after storm damage, new shingle installs  
**FAQ:** How long does replacement take? Do you work with insurance? What brands?

## Mover

**Services:** Local moves, long-distance, packing, commercial, piano/specialty  
**Hero angle:** Stress-free moving in {city} — fixed quotes, no hidden fees  
**Form fields:** move date, from zip, to zip, home size (studio–4br), packing needed  
**Trust:** Licensed movers, insured cargo, background-checked crew  
**FAQ:** Hourly vs flat rate? Deposit? What if date changes?

## Plumber

**Services:** Emergency repairs, drains, water heaters, repiping, fixtures  
**Hero angle:** Same-day plumbing in {city} — 24/7 emergencies  
**Form fields:** urgency (emergency/this week/flexible), issue type, property type  
**Emergency banner:** enabled  
**FAQ:** Service call fee? Warranty on work?

## HVAC

**Services:** AC repair, furnace, install, maintenance plans, duct cleaning  
**Hero angle:** Stay comfortable year-round — tune-ups from $X  
**Form fields:** system type, issue (no cool/no heat/noise), equipment age  
**Seasonal CTA:** Pre-summer AC check / Pre-winter furnace tune-up  

## Landscaper

**Services:** Lawn care, design, hardscape, irrigation, seasonal cleanup  
**Form fields:** lot size, services interested (multi), frequency  
**Gallery:** Required — lawn transformations, patios  

## House cleaner

**Services:** Standard clean, deep clean, move-in/out, recurring  
**Form fields:** sqft, bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency, pets  
**Trust:** Bonded, supplies included, satisfaction guarantee  

## Pest control

**Services:** General pest, termite, rodent, mosquito, commercial  
**Form fields:** pest type, property type, noticed when, pets/children  
**Trust:** Safe for pets, guarantee period  

## General contractor

**Services:** Kitchen/bath remodel, additions, repairs, handyman  
**Form fields:** project type, budget range, timeline, description  

---

## Copy tone (all verticals)

- Plain language, no jargon unless explained
- Mention **local city** 3–5 times naturally across pages
- Specific numbers where believable ("15+ years", "500+ roofs") — only if user confirms
- CTAs: "Get Free Quote", "Schedule Inspection", "Call Now" — not "Submit" or "Learn More" alone
